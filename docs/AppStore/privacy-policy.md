# Widgetdeck Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026
**Developer:** Daniel Radu Betiu (the "developer", "we")
**Contact:** mineprodan@gmail.com

## The short version

Widgetdeck doesn't collect your personal data. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone, and anything that syncs goes through **your own iCloud account**, which we can't see.

## What the app uses, and where it stays

| What | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| **Calendar** (only if you allow it) | To find your classes for the Student widgets | Read on your iPhone. We save event identifiers and your own labels (like course names), never the events themselves. We never change your calendar |
| **Event notes** (part of calendar access) | To suggest a room name (like "ICS 192") when you set up a class | Read on your iPhone only while the app is open. Notes are never saved; only a room you confirm is kept, with the course |
| **Location** (only if you turn on "Leave by", While Using the App) | To estimate travel time to class for leave-by times and alerts | Your current location and the class address are sent to **Apple Maps** for one travel-time estimate. Widgetdeck never stores your location. The resulting travel times are kept on your iPhone for up to 12 hours, labelled by a scrambled code instead of the address |
| **Reminders** (only if you import assignments) | To copy to-dos with a due date from one list you choose | Read on your iPhone. We never change your reminders |
| **Photos you pick** | Photo widget and Theme Sync | Resized copies are stored on your iPhone. We never see your photo library; you choose each photo |
| **Notifications** (only if you allow them) | Class reminders and an optional evening mood reminder | Scheduled on your iPhone |
| **Habits, mood check-ins and notes** | Wellness section and widgets | On your iPhone and in your iCloud private database. Mood notes are end-to-end encrypted by iCloud. We can't access any of it |
| **Battery, storage, heat, CPU and memory readings** | Tech section and widgets | Calculated and stored on your iPhone. Recent readings are kept for 7 days, then deleted |
| **Sync log** | A short record of when data synced and widgets refreshed, to help fix widgets that stop updating. It never includes calendar contents | On your iPhone only (the last 400 lines). It leaves your iPhone only if you share it from **Settings → About → Sync log** |
| **Your courses, themes, reminder settings and widget settings** (including the room, travel mode and leave-by choice you set for a course) | So they appear on your other devices | Synced with Apple's iCloud **private database**, in your iCloud account. We can't access it |

## What we don't do

- We don't sell, share or rent any data.
- We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't track you across apps or websites.
- We don't run our own servers holding your data.

## Network requests the app makes

- **Apple services:** iCloud (CloudKit) sync, and Apple Maps for travel-time estimates when you use "Leave by" (Apple's privacy policy applies to those requests).
- **Feature settings file:** the app downloads a small settings file (`flags.json`) from this app's website, hosted by GitHub Pages (`https://mineprodan.github.io/flags.json`). It sends no personal information. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; we have no access to GitHub's request logs.

## Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. You control this in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements**.

## Exporting your data

**Settings → Export my data** makes one file with your profile, courses, assignments, grades, reminder settings, habits, mood check-ins (with notes) and goals. It's created on your iPhone and goes only where you save or share it.

## Deleting your data

Open **Widgetdeck → Settings → Delete All Data**. This removes the app's data from your iPhone and deletes Widgetdeck's data from your iCloud. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data. You can also manage iCloud data in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**.

## Children

Widgetdeck isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. If a change affects what data leaves your device, we'll mention it in the app's release notes.
